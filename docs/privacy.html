What the app does with your files

The app scans the storage on your device to find identical files and visually similar photos. This happens entirely on your device.

File names, contents, hashes and previews are not uploaded, sold or shared with us or anyone else.

We do not have accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or messages.

Permissions

  • All files access / storage: needed to read files across your storage so duplicates can be found,

    • and to move the copies you select into a trash folder. Nothing is removed without your confirmation.

  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad provider (see below).

Trash folder

Files you remove are moved to a folder named "Cloneout Trash" on your storage. You can restore

them or delete them permanently from within the app. Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version offers optional rewarded ads (you watch a short ad to earn extra free cleanup), served by Google AdMob.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app first asks for your consent through Google's

User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice later in Settings ("Ad privacy choices"). AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID,

IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.

This is handled by Google under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings

(Settings → Google → Ads). Buying Pro removes ads.

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

From version 1.1.0 the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix bugs.

They are switched off until you have answered the consent prompt (where one is required) and only run while

"Share anonymous usage data" in Settings is on; you can turn it off at any time. We receive counts, sizes and file

categories (for example "scan finished, 12 groups, 340 MB"), your plan (free or Pro), app and device model and Android version,

purchase and ad events, and crash or error reports. We never receive file names, folder paths or file contents.

Firebase also uses a random app-instance ID and may use your advertising ID. This is handled by Google under

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made,

not your payment details.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data locally: whether Pro is unlocked and how much free cleanup you

have used. For similar-photo matching it also keeps a small cache of numeric "fingerprints" of your photos

so later scans are faster. The fingerprints cannot be turned back into pictures, never leave your device, and

are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

To keep the free cleanup allowance fair across reinstalls, the app also stores two numbers (how much free

cleanup was used, and ad bonuses) in a small hidden file named `.cloneout_state` in your internal storage,

and in Android's automatic backup of the app (if you have backups on). The file contains no personal

information and can be deleted at any time.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not

knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can uninstall the app at any time, revoke file access in Android settings, and delete the trash folder.
