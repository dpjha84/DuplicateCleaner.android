**Full description (Play allows 4,000 characters; this is about 2,300):**
Running out of space on your phone? Cloneout is a storage cleaner that finds duplicate files, similar photos, large files and WhatsApp media, so you can free up storage in minutes without losing anything by mistake.

DUPLICATE FILE CLEANER
Cloneout finds duplicate files by their content, not by name, so renamed copies are caught too. It works on every file type: duplicate photos, videos, documents, downloads, audio and archives. The original copy is kept automatically (Smart keep), and you only decide when it is unclear.

SIMILAR PHOTOS
Find similar photos: burst shots, resized or re-saved copies and slightly edited versions. Cloneout suggests the best photo to keep, so cleaning your gallery takes seconds. Cleaning similar photos is a Pro feature; finding and previewing them is free.

LARGE FILES FINDER
See the biggest videos, downloads and files on your phone, biggest first. Filter by size (50 MB, 100 MB, 500 MB or 1 GB and up) or by type, preview anything, then pick what to remove. Nothing is selected for you.

WHATSAPP MEDIA CLEANER
WhatsApp quietly saves every photo, video, voice note and document you receive. Cloneout lists them by type and size, including WhatsApp Business, so you can clear the heavy ones. Your chats stay; cleaned media shows as missing inside WhatsApp, and Trash can bring it back.

SAFE AND PRIVATE
- Safe Trash: nothing is deleted straight away; restore any file with one tap, or empty Trash when you are sure
- Preview photos, videos and files before you clean them
- No account or sign-up needed
- Your files never leave your device; everything is scanned on the phone

FREE AND PRO
FREE: unlimited scanning and previews, plus 500 MB of cleanup to start. Watch a short ad for 50 MB more (up to 2 a day).
PRO: one-time purchase, no subscription. Unlimited cleanup, similar-photo cleanup and no ads. Restore it on any device with the same Google account.

PERMISSION
Cloneout needs "All files access" to look through every folder on your device for duplicates, large files and WhatsApp media. Files are only read on your phone and are never uploaded.

Free up storage, remove duplicate photos and clear WhatsApp clutter with Cloneout.
